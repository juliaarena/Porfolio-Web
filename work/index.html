About

Design with real intention.

Hello, I’m Julia Arena, a Product Designer based in Buenos Aires - Argentina. I've always had a curious mind, an eye for detail, and a soft spot for all things art and design.

I’ve spent the last seven years designing digital products and experiences, turning complex problems into things that feel simple, intuitive, and considered. I love understanding how things work — from products and technology to people and the way they think. That curiosity is what drew me to UX, and what keeps me interested in it.

I find inspiration in the little things: the architecture of a building, a clever interaction, a beautifully designed object, or the motion in a film title. I’m drawn to bringing order to chaos, questioning the obvious, and obsessing over the details that make an experience feel just right.
